# Instagram growth plan

## Load

`brand-guardrails`, `docs/instagram/strategy.md`, `docs/instagram/growth-playbook.md`, `docs/instagram/repurposing-system.md`, `docs/strategy/roadmap.md`, `docs/funnel/cta-registry.md`, and the previous month's file in `instagram/calendar/` if any.

## Process

1. **Anchor to YouTube.** List the videos going up in the period (roadmap). Each gets a pack (hook reel, carousel, lesson reel, stories).
2. **Choose one growth lever to test** for four weeks from the playbook (shareable carousels, a recurring series, a "your number" hook, collabs, targeted comments, a DM keyword, story polls, trial reels, YouTube cross-posts). Only one at a time. State the hypothesis and how it is measured.
3. **Hold the mix:** about 60% Ideas, 20% Proof, 20% Life. Life posts are Linh's call. Leave them as slots for Linh to fill, not content to invent.
4. **Build the calendar** with the cadence in the strategy doc (about four feed posts and three to five story days a week). Each row: date, format, pillar, source video, hook line, ask, owner, status.
5. **Write the hooks** for each post using the hook formulas. Real moments only. Use `[CONFIRM WITH LINH]` for a scene that has not been supplied.
6. **Profile tasks** for the period: bio, pinned posts, highlights, link. Tie bio changes to the rebrand date.
7. **Targets.** Propose process metrics (saves, shares, link taps, sign-ups with UTM) and ask the team to set numbers. Do not promise follower numbers.
8. **Review block** at the end of the file for the monthly review.

## Rules

- One ask per post, from the registry.
- Keep a sustainable cadence. If the team is small, drop to three feed posts and hold it eight weeks.
- Do not recommend buying followers, pods or follow/unfollow tactics.
- Never include a client or team member in a post without permission.
- Do not post calculator results until it is live.
- Sensitive topics are Linh's call each time ([open question 10](../../../docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md)).

## Output

Write `instagram/calendar/YYYY-MM.md` using the month template in `instagram/README.md`. Reply with the lever, the three biggest posts, the profile tasks and any questions for Linh.
