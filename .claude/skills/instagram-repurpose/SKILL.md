# Instagram repurpose

## Load

`brand-guardrails`, `docs/instagram/strategy.md`, `docs/instagram/repurposing-system.md`, `templates/instagram-carousel.md`, `templates/instagram-reel.md`, `docs/funnel/cta-registry.md`, and the video's `script.md`.

## Process

1. **Pick the one belief** from the script's header. Everything in the pack serves it.
2. **Choose the pillar** (Accidental vs Intentional, Count it, Free it, My team, Live it, Behind the founder).
3. **Hook reel (20 to 40 s):** the script's hook, tightened. The hook is on screen and spoken in the first 2 seconds.
4. **Lesson reel (45 to 75 s):** the single strongest lesson or story. Pick it by the strongest named person with a number. One point.
5. **Carousel (6 to 8 slides):** hook, the moment, one point per slide, the exercise, the ask. Max 25 words per slide.
6. **Quote or number image:** one direct quote from a team member (only if cleared) or one sourced number.
7. **Story set (3 to 5 frames):** a poll, a question box, the exercise, a link sticker to the CTA.
8. **Caption (150 to 200 words, atomic essay):** hook, the belief flipped, one example, the cost, one step, the ask.
9. **Schedule** against the cadence in the strategy doc. Wed video day, Thu carousel, Sat lesson reel, Mon quote and poll.
10. **Checks:** one belief, one ask per asset (from the registry), facts from the fact sheet, no one featured without permission, captions on every reel, alt text on the carousel.

## Rules

- Reuse the script's own words where possible. Do not invent stories or numbers.
- Disclosure before diagnosis. Never "you're doing it wrong".
- Slide 1 and second 1 must stop a scroll on their own.
- The ask is the registry's CTA. Not YouTube "subscribe".
- Life-content posts (family, faith, sobriety) are Linh's call. Do not generate them from inference.
- Do not announce a calculator result or number before the calculator is live.
- No hype words, no em dashes, Australian/UK spelling.

## Output

Write `videos/YYYY-MM-DD-slug/instagram.md` with each asset under its heading (copy-ready), the posting schedule and a checklist. Summarise in chat: the belief, the asset list and any open confirms.
