# Story bank

The audit found the same examples (the cooking-receipts spreadsheet, the home cook, GoHighLevel) repeated in two or three videos within five weeks. This skill prevents it.

## Load

`docs/process/story-bank.md`, `docs/brand/fact-sheet.md`, `docs/brand/lp-vs-oa.md`, `docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`.

## Modes

### 1. Check a draft

1. List every story, example, quote and number in the script.
2. For each, find it in the bank. Report: story, last used, days since, cleared status, source.
3. Flag: any inside 8 weeks (unless the video is about that story), any not cleared (🟡 or ❓), any without a source, any named person without permission, any client name (LP vs OA).
4. Suggest an unused story from the bank that fits the lesson.

### 2. Log after approval

When the script is approved, add rows to the table: story, who, the point, source, video, date, cleared. Show the rows first, then write them only after the user confirms.

### 3. Find a story for a topic

1. Search the bank for stories that fit the lesson and have not been used in 8 weeks.
2. If none, list the **questions to ask** the team or Linh to surface one. Examples: "What is a moment you said 'once things settle down'?", "What did the team get wrong and fix?", "What number changed?"
3. Never invent a story. Offer a question, not a scene.

## Rules

- A story needs a **source** and a **number or moment**.
- Use first names only. Confirm with the person before they appear.
- Rest a story for 8 weeks unless the video is about it.
- Linh's own stories need her confirmation for sensitive topics (sobriety, faith, family).
- A client story needs written permission and a before and after in the client's words, and stays proof inside a video.
