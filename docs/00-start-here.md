# Start here

Ten minutes to understand what we are building and why.

## The one-line strategy

Own the idea of **The Intentional Founder** on YouTube and Instagram. Reach founders through the problem they already feel (doing it all), move them to the identity (Intentional Founder), and give them one next step into Linh's funnel.

## The umbrella belief

> **Don't just build a successful business. Build a successful life, intentionally.**

Every video plants exactly one logical agreement and one emotional agreement toward this belief. A video that teaches a skill without moving the viewer toward it is off-strategy.

## The pivot in three lines

| From | To |
|---|---|
| Founder Freedom, Freedom Builder | **The Intentional Founder** |
| Freedom as the hook | Freedom as the result, intention as the vehicle |
| Delegation tips | Delegation as the means to a life you chose |

Linh already says it on camera: *"freedom without intention can very quickly become wasted capacity."* Freedom is the pillar. Intention is the vehicle.

**Terminology**

- **Accidental founder**: the villain state. Revenue up, life on hold.
- **Intentional Founder**: who the viewer becomes.
- **Task Delegator trap**: the named villain for delegation content. Keep it.
- **Freedom Builder**: retired. Use "Intentional Founder" from the rename onward.

## Why the channel is built this way

The audit of the first six videos (15 Sep to 12 Oct 2026, small sample, read as direction only) found:

1. **Retention is the leak, not the click.** Viewers stay 30 seconds to 2 minutes on 10 to 18 minute videos. In four videos the "subscribe" pitch started around 0:50, right where viewers left.
2. **"Cost" titles win the click.** Titles naming a cost pulled 3.5% to 3.8% CTR. Delegation and hiring titles sat at 1.8% to 1.9%.
3. **Delegation mechanics win the watch.** "5 Delegation Steps" and "4 Hidden Costs" held viewers longest.
4. **Stories beat claims.** The two best-held videos opened on a person.
5. **Five CTAs in six videos.** The channel builds trust and then hands viewers five different exits.

Full detail: [strategy/channel-audit-findings.md](strategy/channel-audit-findings.md).
