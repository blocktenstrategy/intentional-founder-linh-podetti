# Audience (the Customer)

## Who the channel is for

A **6 to 7 figure founder who already has a team**, looks successful on paper, and is still the one everything comes back to. They have hired before and watched it not stick. The cost shows up at home: a missed dinner, a distracted school pickup, a spouse who has stopped asking how work is going.

Coaching is for established founders at **$200K revenue and above**.

## Who it is not for

Say this out loud in some videos. It sharpens the right viewer's recognition.

- Solo founders with no team yet
- Founders still finding product-market fit
- People who believe burnout is just the price of success

## The belief system underneath

From Linh's own *360 Degree Success* "Common Self-Limiting Beliefs". The viewer carries these without saying them.

| Theme | The belief |
|---|---|
| Rest, time and control | "If I delegate, things won't be done properly." "Rest is lazy. If I'm not grinding, I'm falling behind." |
| Asking for help and vulnerability | "Asking for help means I'm weak." "It's not safe to be vulnerable or show weakness." |
| Perfectionism and fear of failure | "I have to have all the answers before I can start." |

## What this means for how we film

A viewer carrying these beliefs does not respond to being diagnosed from outside ("you're the bottleneck"). It reads as confrontation and triggers the shame response that makes people click away. They respond to **recognition**.

**Rule: disclosure before diagnosis.**

1. Linh names the pattern in herself first (a real moment, a real cost).
2. Then she invites the viewer to see themselves in it.
3. Then she gives one step.

Linh has already done this well in print. Her chapter on quitting drinking opens with her own "shame, guilt, and regret" before it speaks to the reader.

## The moments we write toward

These come from the website and are the viewer's own scenes. Use them as hook material.

- On a client call during a family holiday
- At a child's school event, texting the team under the table
- Dinner with the phone face up
- Health, marriage and friendships "on hold"
- Saying "once I hire the right person", "once things settle down", "once we hit the next number" for years

## Two camps (from the sample script)

When talking about a new tool or method, the viewer sits in one of two camps. Name both.

1. Haven't started, because it feels overwhelming.
2. Have played with it, but it hasn't changed how the business runs.
