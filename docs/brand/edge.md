# Edge (what only Linh can say)

Block Ten's CEO framework reads **Customer, Edge, Offer**. For this channel, Edge is lived experience plus proof, not a credential list.

## Four proof points no content-only competitor can claim

1. **She built the thing she teaches.** Outsourcing Angel since 2015, 2,000+ founders' businesses staffed, a 4.9★ rating and a 5-year average team member tenure.
2. **She has the scar tissue, not just the framework.** A broke single mum at 20 to a 7-figure founder with a 100+ person team. It is documented in *360 Degree Success*, not borrowed.
3. **She covers both halves.** The operational side (delegation, staffing, systems) and the identity side (limiting beliefs, the 360° framework), in the same voice. Most delegation content is all systems or all mindset.
4. **She leads with vulnerability and it lands.** Panic attacks were her wake-up call. She discloses first, then the reader recognises themselves.

## How we use Edge in a script

- Every pillar leads with **Linh's own account of a pattern** before it describes the viewer's.
- Proof (client outcomes, the 5-year tenure stat, press) is **supporting evidence inside a video**, never the premise. A standalone case-study video reads as Outsourcing Angel content.
- Named team stories are Linh's strongest proof and competitors cannot copy them. Rotate them and log use in the [story bank](../process/story-bank.md).
