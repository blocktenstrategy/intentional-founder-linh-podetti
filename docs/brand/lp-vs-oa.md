# Linh Podetti's channel vs Outsourcing Angel's channel

This is **Linh Podetti's** channel. It is not Outsourcing Angel's.

| | Linh Podetti (this channel) | Outsourcing Angel |
|---|---|---|
| Frames the problem as | A leadership and life problem | An operations and staffing problem |
| Teaches | Why she believes what she believes, and how she personally thinks about handing work off | Staffing, role design and the delegation mechanics |
| Voice | First person, disclosure first | Company voice |
| The ask | Clarity for the founder (calculator, quiz, Freedom Audit) | Staffing consultations |
| Client stories | Proof **inside** a video | Can be the premise of a video |

## The rule

> If a video reads as "here's OA's exact process" or "here's what my staffing company does step by step", it belongs on Outsourcing Angel's channel.

## Practical guardrails

1. **Client case studies and results are never a standalone upload here.** Fold them into a video as evidence.
2. **The delegation and staffing pillar is the one most at risk of drifting.** Test: is this *why Linh believes it* and *how she thinks*, or *how OA does it*?
3. **The ask stays at the mindset and planning level.** The quiz, the calculator and the Freedom Audit are framed as clarity for the founder, not as a sales path into an Outsourcing Angel package.
4. **An Outsourcing Angel mention is a soft line, never the primary CTA.** If it appears, it is one sentence after the primary ask.
5. **Examples come from Linh's own team and life, not OA clients**, until a client has given written permission and a before/after in their own words.

## The sample script and this rule

The sample script (team AI huddle) uses Outsourcing Angel's team as the story source and ends with a soft Outsourcing Angel line after the primary quiz CTA. That is acceptable under rule 4.
