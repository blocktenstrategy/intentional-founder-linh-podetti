# Positioning: The Intentional Founder

## The definition (use word for word)

> An **Intentional Founder** is a business owner who decides what a good life looks like first, then builds a business that makes room for it.

Say "defined by Linh Podetti, author of *360 Degree Success*". Do not say "coined by" until Linh confirms ([open question 2](../OPEN-QUESTIONS.md)).

## One-sentence positioning

Linh Podetti helps founders who have already built a real business stop building their life by accident, and design both on purpose.

## The umbrella belief

**Don't just build a successful business. Build a successful life, intentionally.**

## The three-part story the brand tells

| Stage | Name | What it means | Where it appears |
|---|---|---|---|
| Villain state | **Accidental success / the accidental founder** | Revenue up, team growing, people know your name, and health, marriage, kids, friendships and faith are on hold | Hook and problem sections |
| Method | **Count it. Free it. Live it.** | See how much time is really yours, take yourself out of the day-to-day, invest the time on purpose | Funnel, calculator, coaching |
| Identity | **The Intentional Founder** | Decides the life first, then builds the business to fit | Brand name, close of videos |

The website also frames it as **Decide, Design, Deliver**. Use whichever frame the video's pillar fits. Do not mix both in one video.

## Why "Intentional Founder" and not "Founder Freedom"

- Nobody owns "intentional founder" on YouTube today (audit search: small podcasts and one 49-view video). It is open to claim.
- It has **no search demand**. It is the brand and the identity, never the keyword. Titles stay in the problem the viewer already types: delegate, bottleneck, remote team, doing everything yourself.
- "Freedom" alone is too generic to carry a title. None of the competitor breakouts used it as the hook, and the one that did underperformed.
- Freedom is the **result**. Intention is the **vehicle**.

## The five-part test for any piece of content

1. Does it open on a person or a moment, not a claim?
2. Does it plant one belief toward the umbrella belief?
3. Would a tired founder stop scrolling for the title and thumbnail?
4. Does the viewer get one thing to do tomorrow?
5. Does it end on the one approved ask?

## What the brand is not

- Not a lifestyle-business brand. Many Intentional Founders run large, growing companies. Linh leads a global team of 100+. The difference is the order of decisions: life first, then the business.
- Not an anti-ambition brand. Being intentional is about direction, not speed.
- Not Outsourcing Angel. See [lp-vs-oa.md](lp-vs-oa.md).
