# Voice and tone

Derived from the sample script and the website copy. When in doubt, read [the sample script](../../examples/2026-11-04-ai-team-lessons/script.md) aloud.

## The voice in five words

**Warm. Plain. Honest. Practical. Unhurried.**

Linh is a founder talking to another founder across a table. She has been where the viewer is. She does not perform expertise.

## How she writes (and speaks)

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| First person ("I", "my team") | "One should", "founders must" |
| Short sentences. One idea each. | Stacked clauses and abstractions |
| Contractions (I'm, don't, it's) | Formal "do not", "it is" |
| Concrete numbers and names ("about 17 hours a month", "Heidi") | "A lot of time", "a team member" |
| A real moment ("while I was driving") | "In today's fast-paced world" |
| Admit what went wrong ("it got things wrong") | Only success stories |
| One exercise per section | Advice with no step to take |
| Australian/UK spelling and "mum" | US spelling |

## Patterns that make it sound like Linh

- **Open on a small, specific moment**, then widen. ("Today, while I was driving, I asked my AI assistant...")
- **Name the two camps** before teaching. ("Either they haven't started... or they've played with it...")
- **"Here's the part I want you to hear."** Pause before the emotional point.
- **Quote the team member** in their own words, then Linh's own version. ("I do this myself too.")
- **Close each section on a plain synthesis line.** ("That's what buying back time looks like.")
- **Say what it cost.** The time saved matters less than the stress or the missed dinner.
- **"Your exercise:"** a task in one to three sentences, usable that day.
- **Be honest about limits.** "It took her more than a day to build... it's never finished."

## Banned or limited

- Opening with "Welcome to the channel", "In today's video", "Hey guys" or a subscribe ask
- Hype: game-changer, unlock, supercharge, 10x, hack, secret, "crush it"
- "Leverage" as a verb, "synergy", "ecosystem", "paradigm"
- Diagnosing the viewer first ("You're the bottleneck")
- Em dashes. Use a colon, a full stop or a comma.
- Fear-selling. "Drain, Depression, Divorce, Disease, Death" is used on the website to name the cost of accidental success. In video, name one real cost through a real moment. Never list all five as a threat.
- Medical, legal or financial advice. Linh shares her experience, not prescriptions.
- Hustle-shaming. Hustle culture is a named opponent, but the viewer is never the target.

## Emotional range by section

| Section | Feel |
|---|---|
| Hook | Curious, a little wry |
| Intro | Warm, credible, brief |
| Teaching | Clear, grounded, specific |
| The cost | Quiet, honest, slower |
| Exercise | Direct, encouraging |
| CTA | Light. An invitation, not a pitch. |

## Terms to use

| Use | Instead of |
|---|---|
| Intentional Founder | Freedom Builder (retired at the rename) |
| Accidental success, accidental founder | "Burnout founder", "workaholic" |
| Task Delegator trap | "Bad delegator" |
| Free years, free weekends | "Spare time" |
| Team member | Staff, resource, "offshore worker" |
