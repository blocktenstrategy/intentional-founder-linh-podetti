## Where the CTA appears (every video)

1. **Spoken once, near the end** (CTA section of the script).
2. **First line of the description**, above the fold.
3. **Pinned comment.**
4. **On-screen text** at the spoken moment.

Never in the first 3 minutes. The "subscribe" block is out of the intro. If used, it is one sentence after the first payoff, around minute 3.

## Script wording (pick the one that matches the live CTA)

**Newsletter**
> If you'd like one idea a week on building a business and a life on purpose, join my newsletter. The link is in the description.

**Free Years Calculator**
> If you'd like to see how much of your remaining time is actually yours, take two minutes with my Free Years Calculator. The link is in the description.

**360° Quiz** (second-touch ask, for a trust-built video)
> If you'd like to see how you're tracking across your business and your life, take my free 360° Quiz. The link is in the description.

## Secondary lines (never a second CTA)

| Line | Rule |
|---|---|
| Comment prompt | One question tied to the exercise. Not a CTA. Always allowed. |
| Next video | One named video, on screen and in the description. Replaces "thanks for watching, subscribe". |
| Outsourcing Angel | One soft sentence after the primary ask, only when the topic is team building. Never the only ask. |
| Intentional Year | A one-line P.S. in Nov and Dec videos, in the description and pinned comment only. |
| Freedom Audit | Only on a video that has already built heavy trust (origin, "stopped being the bottleneck"). It replaces the primary CTA. It does not sit beside it. |
