# Funnel map

The path from a cold viewer to a coaching conversation. The channel's job is the first step only.

```
        YouTube video  /  Instagram reel, carousel, story
                         |  ONE call to action
                         v
   ┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │ 1. Free Years Calculator  (2 min)        │  ← newsletter until it is live
   │    "How many free years do you have?"    │
   └──────────────────────────────────────────┘
                         v   email gate (name, email, who you are)
   ┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │ 2. Results page                          │
   │    free years, free weekends, report     │
   └──────────────────────────────────────────┘
                         v
   ┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │ 3. 360° Quiz  /  Bottleneck Identifier   │  second touch, low friction
   └──────────────────────────────────────────┘
                         v
   ┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │ 4. Freedom Audit (free 45 to 60 min call)│  the ONLY "book a call" ask
   │    or Coaching application               │
   └──────────────────────────────────────────┘
                         v
   The Intentional Founder Blueprint (1:1 coaching, founders at $200K+)
```

## The Count it. Free it. Live it. spine

| Step | Meaning | Content that serves it |
|---|---|---|
| **Count it** | See how much time is really yours | Free years, the cost of doing it all, accidental success |
| **Free it** | Take yourself out of the day-to-day | Delegation, hiring, remote teams, systems, AI |
| **Live it** | Invest the time on purpose | 360° areas, enough, family, health, faith, experiences |

Map every video to one step. A channel with only "Free it" content brings delegation searchers. A channel with only "Live it" content loses the search traffic. Balance by cluster (see [playlists](../strategy/playlists-and-clusters.md)).

## Asks, in order of friction

| Order | Ask | Friction | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Free Years Calculator (or newsletter while not live) | Lowest. Emotional, curiosity-driven. | Top-of-funnel entry |
| 2 | 360° Quiz or Bottleneck Identifier | Low to medium. A diagnostic, not a pitch. | Second touch |
| 3 | Freedom Audit | Highest. A free call ending in a written plan. | The only "book a call" ask |

**Which ask goes in a video** depends on how much trust that specific video has built, not on the pillar. A cold-traffic search video asks for the lowest friction step. A story video can ask for the next one.

## Where Instagram fits

Instagram is the **identity and whole-life layer**. YouTube is the **search and depth layer**. Instagram sends people to the same first step. See [instagram/strategy.md](../instagram/strategy.md).

## What to measure at each step

| Step | Metric | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Video | CTR, average view duration, subscribers per 1K views | YouTube Studio |
| CTA | Clicks from description and pinned comment | UTM links, analytics |
| Calculator | Starts, email captures, completion | Site analytics |
| Audit and coaching | Applications and booked calls | CRM |

UTM convention: `utm_source=youtube|instagram`, `utm_medium=video|reel|carousel|bio`, `utm_campaign=YYYY-MM-DD-slug`.
