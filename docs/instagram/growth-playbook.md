# Instagram growth playbook

Account at 1,381 followers. The goal is **qualified** followers (founders with a team) who move into the funnel, not a big number. These are tests to run, not guarantees. Review each at 4 and 8 weeks.

## The loop

```
Reel or carousel (idea) → shares and saves → new founders find the account
→ profile (bio, highlights, pinned posts) → link: Free Years Calculator
→ newsletter → YouTube for depth → Freedom Audit
```

## Growth levers (test one at a time for 4 weeks)

| # | Lever | Why it may work | How to test |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Shareable carousels** ("Accidental vs Intentional", "Count your free weekends") | Founders share what describes them | Track shares and saves per carousel |
| 2 | **A recurring reel series** ("What I stopped doing" or "A real day") | Series builds a reason to follow | 6 episodes, same format, compare against ad hoc reels |
| 3 | **A "your number" hook** (free years, free weekends) | A personal number is shareable and curiosity-driven | Post the maths, ask for the number in comments |
| 4 | **Collabs and tags** with EO members, podcast hosts, founders Linh has worked with | Borrow warm audiences | 2 collab posts a month; track new followers per collab |
| 5 | **Comment on 10 target accounts a day** (founder, EO, leadership accounts) with a real, specific reply | Linh is seen by the audience she wants | 15 minutes a day, log what gets profile visits |
| 6 | **DM keyword** ("comment FREE YEARS") | Moves a viewer from passive to a lead | Needs an automation tool; track keyword count and sign-ups |
| 7 | **Stories with polls and question boxes** | Builds two-way contact and shows up in the feed order | 3 story days a week, one interactive sticker each |
| 8 | **Trial reels** to non-followers first, then main grid for what works | Lower risk testing | Post 2 hooks per idea as trial reels |
| 9 | **Cross-post from YouTube** (Shorts and clips) with the Instagram-native caption | Reuses video work | Compare watch-through vs native reels |

## What not to do

- Do not buy followers, engagement pods or follow/unfollow tactics. They fill the account with non-founders.
- Do not post a calculator result before it is live.
- Do not share a client's name, results or photo without permission.
- Do not post every day for a month and burn out. Hold the cadence you can sustain for eight weeks.
- Do not turn the whole feed into promotion. Stay near 60/20/20 across ideas, proof and life.
