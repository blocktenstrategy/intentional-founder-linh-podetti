# Instagram strategy

Instagram [@linh.podetti](https://www.instagram.com/linh.podetti/) is the **identity and whole-life layer** of The Intentional Founder. YouTube is the search and depth layer. Both send people to the same first step.

**Read:** the account already has what the brand needs: Linh's whole life in one place. It is positioned for "freedom", not "intentional". The bio and highlight names should change at the [rebrand](../strategy/rebrand-checklist.md).

## The job of Instagram

| YouTube does | Instagram does |
|---|---|
| Answers a problem someone searches for | Makes a founder feel *seen* in a scroll |
| Teaches in 8 to 12 minutes | Delivers one idea in 30 to 90 seconds or 6 to 8 slides |
| Builds trust through depth | Builds trust through frequency and personality |
| Cold traffic from search | Warm traffic from follows, shares and DMs |

Instagram does the thing YouTube cannot: show the **life** half of "Intentional Founder". Family dinner, a morning, a trip, a real day. The business half proves she is a founder. The life half proves she practices what she says.

## The three content layers

| Layer | Share of posts | What it is | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Ideas** (60%) | Carousels and reels | One belief from a video, in the brand's frame | "Accidental founder vs Intentional Founder" table, "Count your free weekends", "3 things to delegate first" |
| **2. Proof** (20%) | Reels, stories, single image | Team stories, client moments, behind the scenes of the business | A team member quote, a weekly huddle, "the AI lessons my team taught me" |
| **3. Life** (20%) | Reels, stories, photos | Family, faith, fitness, travel, honest moments | A phone face-down at dinner, a trip, a quiet morning |

**Rule: life content is real, not staged for the funnel.** Disclose only what Linh is happy to share. Sobriety and faith are Linh's choice each time ([open question 10](../OPEN-QUESTIONS.md)).

## The funnel link

One link in bio. One ask in every post. The ask matches the [CTA registry](../funnel/cta-registry.md).

| Where | Ask |
|---|---|
| Link in bio | The Free Years Calculator (newsletter until live), with the label "Count your free years" |
| Reel and carousel last frame | "Count your free years. Link in bio." or the comment keyword |
| Stories | The poll or question sticker, then a link sticker to the calculator |
| Comments | One reply thread per post, answered by Linh |

Optional comment keyword: "Comment **FREE YEARS** and I'll send you the calculator." Needs a DM automation tool, so confirm which tool the team uses.

## Cadence (starting point, review monthly)

| Day | Post |
|---|---|
| Mon | Reel (Ideas) |
| Tue | Stories (poll or question) |
| Wed | Carousel (Ideas) on the day the video goes up |
| Thu | Reel (Proof or Life) |
| Fri | Stories (behind the scenes) |
| Sat or Sun | One Life post |

About four feed posts and three to five story days a week. If that is too much, drop to three feed posts (carousel, reel, life) and keep it for eight weeks before judging.

## What success looks like (set targets with the team)

Measure these, not follower count alone:

- Profile visits → link taps → calculator or newsletter sign-ups
- Saves and shares per post (the signals that spread an idea)
- DMs and comment keywords
- Followers who are founders with a team (qualitative: who is following)

Review in the [analytics review](../process/analytics-review.md) once a month.

## Rules

1. Every post is built on **one belief**, like a video.
2. Hook in the first line (carousel slide 1, reel second 1).
3. One ask per post, from the registry.
4. Disclosure before diagnosis. Never "you're doing it wrong".
5. Facts from the [fact sheet](../brand/fact-sheet.md).
6. No team member or client appears without their permission.
