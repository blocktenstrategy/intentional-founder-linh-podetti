# Analytics review

A weekly 15-minute check and a monthly 45-minute review. The channel is small (52 subscribers, 11 videos), so numbers are noisy. Read direction, not proof, and change one thing at a time.

## Weekly (15 minutes, day 7 of each video)

Record in the video's `results.md`:

| Metric | Where | Healthy direction |
|---|---|---|
| Impressions | Studio → Reach | Rising across videos |
| **CTR** | Studio → Reach | Above 3% on cost titles. Under 2% at 72 hours: swap the thumbnail. |
| **Average view duration** | Studio → Engagement | The leak. The first six videos ran 30 to 131 seconds. |
| **% viewed** | Studio → Engagement | Above 15% would be a clear win at this stage |
| **Retention graph** | Per video | Look for a cliff between 0:45 and 1:10 |
| Subscribers and **subs per 1K views** | Studio | About 17 per 1K was the first-six average |
| Traffic sources | Studio → Reach | Check for outside spikes |
| CTA clicks | UTM / analytics | The only number that matters for the funnel |

## Monthly review

1. Update the table below.
2. Name the **best and worst video** and the reason (hook, title, thumbnail, topic, length).
3. Check the **cluster mix** against the [target](../strategy/content-pillars.md).
4. Check **CTA performance**: clicks per 1,000 views.
5. Review **Instagram** ([growth playbook](../instagram/growth-playbook.md)).
6. Choose **one change** for next month. Write it down. Only one.
7. Update the [roadmap](../strategy/roadmap.md) and [CTA registry](../funnel/cta-registry.md) if needed.

## Decision rules

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| CTR under 2% at 72 hours | Swap the thumbnail, then the title, one at a time |
| Average view under 60 s | Rewrite the first 60 seconds of the next script |
| Watch time up, subs flat | Move the ask to after a payoff in the next video |
| Subs per 1K views up | Make more in that cluster |
| A video fades after week 1 | Do not reshoot. Learn from the topic and the hook. |
| Two videos in a cluster underperform | Pause that cluster for two weeks and check the audience question |

## Reading Studio exports

If you export a CSV, use the `analytics-review` skill. It reads the file, joins it to the roadmap and returns three actions.

## What not to do

- Do not judge a video before day 7.
- Do not compare against the large channels. Compare each video to the previous three.
- Do not change the thumbnail, the title and the hook in the same week.
- Do not chase a spike without checking the traffic source.
