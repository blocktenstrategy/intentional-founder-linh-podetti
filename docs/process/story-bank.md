# Story bank

Stories and examples are Linh's strongest proof, and the audit found them repeating: the cooking-receipts spreadsheet, the home cook and GoHighLevel each appeared in two or three videos within five weeks. This file tracks every story so none is used twice in a short window.

**Rules**

1. Check this file before writing a script. Log a story the day the script is approved.
2. Do not reuse a story within **8 weeks** unless the video is *about* that story.
3. Use first names only. Confirm with the person before a new story goes in. Mark `Cleared` once confirmed.
4. A story needs a **source** (recording, interview, book) and a **number or a moment**.
5. Add three or more new stories a month (ask the team in the huddle, as in the sample script).
