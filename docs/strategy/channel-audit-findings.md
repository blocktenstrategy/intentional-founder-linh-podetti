Condensed from the audit "The Intentional Founder Pivot". **Sample size is small** (774 views and 13 subscribers in 28 days, 15 Sep to 12 Oct). Read every number as direction, not proof.

The channel is winning on one idea: **the hidden cost of being the founder who does everything.** Build the next 17 weeks around that, frame it as "Accidental vs Intentional Founder", and **fix retention before anything else.**

## The leak: the first 60 seconds

- In four of six videos the "this channel is for you, subscribe" block starts 47 to 62 seconds in. The average viewer leaves at 51 to 66 seconds.
- The one video with **no pitch** in its intro (5 Delegation Steps) holds viewers more than twice as long.
- **Stories beat claims.** The two best-held videos open on a person (Linh's panic attacks, Gail). The two weakest open on an abstract claim.
- Two titles promise something the video does not deliver early. A good click followed by a broken promise is the classic retention drop.

## Other patterns

- **Linh already says the pivot on camera:** "freedom without intention can very quickly become wasted capacity".
- **She already has a villain and a hero.** "Task Delegator" vs "Freedom Builder" runs through four videos. Keep Task Delegator trap. Replace Freedom Builder with Intentional Founder from the rename.
- **Five different CTAs in six videos:** the newsletter, the Bottleneck Quiz, the 360° Success Quiz, "reach out to Outsourcing Angel" (and one video had two).
- **Facts drift:** tenure appeared as "17 years", "more than 15 years", "more than a decade". See [fact sheet](../brand/fact-sheet.md).
- **Examples repeat:** the cooking-receipts spreadsheet, the home cook and GoHighLevel each appear in two or three videos within five weeks. See [story bank](../process/story-bank.md).
- **Every video closes the same way:** "thanks for watching, subscribe, see you next video". Point to one named next video instead.

## Fixes, ranked by impact

1. **Rebuild the first 60 seconds of every video.** Pattern interrupt in the first 3 seconds (a result or a sharp claim, never a welcome). Confirm the click by second 10. Name the common belief, flip it, open a loop paid off later. Teach the second-best point first and the best second (2-1-3-4 order). Take the channel pitch out of the intro. Open on a person.
2. **Shorter cuts: 8 to 12 minutes.** Four of six videos ran 13 to 18.5 minutes. At current retention, length only adds drop-off. Keep chapters, but make chapter names reasons to keep watching.
3. **One CTA per video, the same one everywhere.** See [CTA registry](../funnel/cta-registry.md).
4. **Rebrand hygiene, all on one day.** See [rebrand checklist](rebrand-checklist.md).
5. **Link every video to the next in its cluster.** End screen plus one description line.
