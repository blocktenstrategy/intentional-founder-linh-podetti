# Competitor playbook

Competitor research is for learning what works, spotting gaps and getting inspired. It is not for copying. Use this when picking an angle or checking a title.

## What the real breakouts share

A breakout here means views well above the channel's own subscriber count. Only two of seven sent were true breakouts for the niche: **Kasim Aslam** and **Cameron England**, both delegation videos from small channels.

**The breakouts all promise a system the viewer can copy, told in the first person, with a business-size number.** Linh has all three and was not yet using them.

None of the breakouts uses "freedom" as its hook. The one that did (Sunny Lenarduzzi, "freedom as an entrepreneur & grew to 8 figures") underperformed for her channel. The pull was "8 figures", not "freedom".

| Video | Channel (subs) | Views | What Linh can take |
|---|---|---|---|
| This Delegation System Changed My Business FOREVER | Kasim Aslam (2.73K) | 2,552 | A concrete weekly system for an assistant → W23 |
| I Delegated My Entire $3M/Yr Agency | Cameron England (14.3K) | 4,044 | First person + a dollar figure + a total outcome. Linh's confirmed 100+ team, 7-figure and nearly 15 years fit. |
| The #1 Mistake Slowing Your Business Growth | Layla at ProcessDriven (152K) | 13,004 | Same idea as Linh's "Chief Approver". 6 minutes long. |
| Own Your Time | Presence & Path (268K) | 20,613 | Whole-life angle told as a story. Model for 360° videos: open on one person's collapse. |
| Everyone is Staff Engineer / Architect Now! | Java Brains (703K) | 241,969 | Off-niche. Use the format: an identity shift caused by AI → W24 |
| How to find true freedom, in 60 seconds | Mark Darwin (118K) | 6,177 | Shorts reference only |
| How i created FREEDOM as an entrepreneur | Sunny Lenarduzzi (796K) | 27,678 | Underperformed. Supports the pivot: freedom alone is too generic for a title. |

## Format ideas by tier

**Large channels (500K+)**: Valuetainment, Ali Abdaal, Hormozi, Dan Martell, Codie Sanchez, BigDeal.

| Idea | Why |
|---|---|
| "The first 5 things to outsource before you hire anyone locally" | Reframes Hormozi's hiring-order format around offshore delegation |
| "Why I Have a 100+ Person Team" | Ali Abdaal's "12 full-time employees" format at scale |
| "Buy Back Your Time" with a twist | Closest positioning match is Dan Martell. Engage head on. |
| "How I run a 100+ person team without losing my family" (Short) | Mirrors Codie Sanchez's 4.8M-view Short |
| A "Tuesday or Funeral Test" equivalent | From BigDeal's "Without Hating Your Life". Build a staffing-specific test. |

**Small channels (<500K)**: EntreLeadership, Foundr, Deya, ProcessDriven, airSlate, Lazy Entrepreneur, Outsourced.

| Idea | Why |
|---|---|
| "Should You Hire a Virtual Assistant?" | EntreLeadership's version is thin (2.9K views on 422K subs). Real search intent. |
| "$0 to $X" origin story | Foundr's format, applied to Linh's broke-single-mum arc |
| "Here's what that video leaves out" response to "How I Build Systems" | Deya's premise is nearly identical. Strong wedge. |
| "What Is [concept]?" evergreen explainer | airSlate's one hit is a plain definition. Low competition. |
| Hire a VA from the Philippines | Lazy Entrepreneur's breakout is almost the OA premise. Proves the intent. Careful: this is OA territory. |

## Open lanes

- **"Intentional founder" has no owner on YouTube.** It has no search demand either. Brand, not keyword.
- **Delegation search results are old.** Valuetainment and EntreLeadership from 8 to 9 years ago dominate. Fresh, specific delegation content has room.
- **Outsourced is a faceless company channel.** Linh has what they don't: a founder people can root for.

## Rules of use

1. Adapt the **format**, not the title.
2. Check the topic against [LP vs OA](../brand/lp-vs-oa.md). Staffing-mechanics ideas often belong to OA.
3. Before adopting a format, record it in the video brief with the source video and views ÷ subs.
