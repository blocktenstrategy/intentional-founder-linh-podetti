# Content pillars and clusters

Two layers. **Pillars** describe what Linh talks about. **Clusters** are how those topics are grouped into playlists after the audit. Use clusters to plan, pillars to check coverage.

## The five pillars (from the brand and content strategy report)

| # | Pillar | For | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Founder Freedom: Start Here** | Founders who built a real business but are still the bottleneck: 6 to 7 figures on paper, missing dinners, distracted at pickup | Why "just work harder" doesn't fix it, and what does. Rename to the Intentional Founder pillar at the rebrand. |
| 2 | **My Delegation and Staffing Systems** | Linh's own approach, philosophy, rules | **The pillar most at risk of drifting into OA's channel.** It is *why Linh believes it* and *how she personally thinks*. Never "OA's exact process". |
| 3 | **Business Systems and Automation** | What to automate, what to delegate, what never to hand off, even with AI | Decisions first, tech second. |
| 4 | **Hiring for Founders** | Practical guidance for founders who've tried and watched it not work | From first outsourced hire to a team member who thinks without you. |
| 5 | **Time, Money and the Cost of "Success"** | What a 7-figure business costs if you're not careful | Linh's journey, the S3Cs, the 360 Degree Success Framework. |

## The five clusters (from the audit, the planning unit)

Cluster around the problem the audience already feels (the cost of doing it all), then move them to the identity (Intentional Founder). That order matches the data and the pivot.

| Cluster | Evidence it works | Playlist name | Funnel step |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The cost of doing it all** | Highest CTR on the channel (3.5% to 3.8%) | The Real Cost of Doing It All | Count it |
| **Delegate and get out of the way** | Best watch time; all real competitor breakouts; real search demand | Delegate, Hire & Lead a Remote Team | Free it |
| **Systems, AI and staying ahead** | Best subscribers per view (one video) | Systems, AI & Staying Ahead | Free it |
| **The Intentional Founder (identity, whole life)** | Nothing published yet. This is the pivot. | Start Here: The Intentional Founder | Live it |
| **360° Success** | Launches January | 360° Success: A Life, Not Just a Business | Live it |

**Pillar to cluster:** P5 → Cost of doing it all. P2 and P4 → Delegate. P3 → Systems and AI. P1 → The Intentional Founder. 360° Success is new.

## Search vs identity

| Layer | Used as | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Problem keywords** | Title and description | delegate, bottleneck, remote team, doing everything yourself, virtual assistant, automate |
| **Identity** | Brand, hook, close, playlist | Intentional Founder, accidental founder |

"Intentional founder" has no search demand. Never lead a title with it.

## Mix per month (target)

Out of four uploads: **two** from Delegate, **one** from Cost of doing it all or Systems/AI, **one** from The Intentional Founder or 360° Success. Adjust after each [analytics review](../process/analytics-review.md).

## The one-belief rule

Every video plants exactly one logical agreement and one emotional agreement toward the umbrella belief. The roadmap lists both for every slot.

Emotions used so far: relief, vindication, hope, identity, aspiration, belonging, urgency. Do not repeat the same emotion in three videos in a row.

## Never

- A standalone client case study (belongs to Outsourcing Angel).
- "What Is Founder Freedom?" as a standalone video. It is absorbed into the Intentional Founder identity video.
- A framework launch before the audience exists (the 5D Freedom Staffing System and the 360° pillars are introduced after traction, as belief first and framework second).
