# Roadmap

The schedule from the channel audit (W8 to W24), with Wednesday upload dates. It **supersedes** the earlier 13-week plan in the strategy report, which started before the audit data existed.

**Last reviewed:** 2026-11-04. Update the Status column whenever a video goes live.

**Gate key**

- **S** Specificity: a number, name, result or villain
- **A** Action: the viewer can do or avoid something tomorrow
- **P** Phone-feed: would a tired founder stop scrolling

A failed gate means use the suggested title or angle. Details in [titles.md](../youtube/titles.md).

## Rules for changing the schedule

- Every slot must have a title, a one-belief line and a gate result before filming.
- Do not publish two videos from the same cluster back to back unless the second answers the first.
- Do not repeat the same story, example or emotion across consecutive videos. Check the [story bank](../process/story-bank.md).
- When a slot changes, update the **Status** here, the [playlists](playlists-and-clusters.md) and the video folder.
