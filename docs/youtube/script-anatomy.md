# Script anatomy

How every script is built. Derived from the sample script, [How My Team Actually Uses AI (5 Lessons for Founders)](../../examples/2026-11-04-ai-team-lessons/script.md). Section-by-section commentary is in the [annotated breakdown](../../examples/2026-11-04-ai-team-lessons/annotated-breakdown.md). Blank version: [templates/script-template.md](../../templates/script-template.md).

## The shape

| Section | Time | Words (approx.) | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Header** | n/a | n/a | Title options, thumbnail text options, source, format, one-belief line |
| **Hook** | 0:00 to 0:25 | 120 | A real moment, a result, the promise |
| **Intro** | 0:25 to 1:00 | 130 | Who Linh is, who this is for, the two camps, how many lessons |
| **Lesson 1 to N** | 1:00 to ~9:45 | 250 to 350 each | Story, cost, quote, Linh's own version, synthesis, exercise |
| **Recap** | ~9:45 to 10:15 | 100 | Numbered list in the imperative, then the thesis line |
| **CTA** | ~10:15 to 10:45 | 100 | One primary ask, one comment question, one named next video |

Total target: **8 to 12 minutes, about 1,500 to 1,800 words** at roughly 160 words a minute. The audit recommends 8 to 12 minutes at current retention. Add on-screen text and b-roll cues in brackets.

## Header (before the script text)

```
Title options (3)
Thumbnail text options (3)
Source: where the story came from (recording, huddle, interview, date)
Format: educational talking-head | story | Q&A, length, word count
Belief: logical agreement + emotional agreement
Cluster: and the funnel step (Count it | Free it | Live it)
CTA: from the registry
```

## Hook (0:00 to 0:25)

1. **Line 1: a specific moment in Linh's own life.** ("Today, while I was driving, I asked my AI assistant...") Concrete, a place, an action.
2. **Line 2 to 3: the result, in plain words.** What happened, what she did not have to do.
3. **Line 4: widen to why it matters** and what the team or the audience did.
4. **Last line: the promise.** "So today I'm passing on those lessons to you."
5. An `[ON SCREEN TEXT]` cue that states the proof ("Real lessons from a real team").

Never open with a welcome, a subscribe ask or a definition. See [hooks-and-retention.md](hooks-and-retention.md).

## Intro (0:25 to 1:00)

- **"Hey, I'm Linh."** One sentence of credibility: founder of Outsourcing Angel, author of *360 Degree Success*, and the brand line ("I help founders become Intentional Founders: you design your life on purpose, then build a business that makes room for it").
- **The two camps.** Name where the viewer might be.
- **Why this is credible.** It is not theory.
- **"Here are [N] lessons."**

No channel pitch. Keep to about 35 seconds.

## Lessons (the repeating block)

Each lesson has the same seven beats:

1. **Action-framed title.** A verb the viewer can do ("Start with one boring, repeated job", "Treat AI like a direct report, not a magic button"). With a timestamp range.
2. **A named person, a real before and after.** Specific numbers ("about 17 hours a month", "about 80%").
3. **The emotional benefit under the practical one.** ("The biggest benefit wasn't just the time. It was her stress and anxiety.")
4. **A direct quote** from the person, in their words.
5. **Linh's own version.** ("I do this myself too.")
6. **A plain synthesis line.** ("That's what buying back time looks like.")
7. **"Your exercise:"** one to three sentences, doable that day.

**Add where the topic allows:** one lesson that is honest about what went wrong. In the sample it is Lesson 2 ("what it got wrong"). It builds trust and is the "most honest part".

Cues: `[ON SCREEN TEXT: ...]` for the one line you want to land, `[B-ROLL: ...]` for visuals.

## Recap (about 30 seconds)

- Numbered list, one line per lesson, **in the imperative** ("Start with one boring, repeated job").
- Then a **thesis line** that ties back to the brand idea: "AI won't give you your life back on its own. But used intentionally, it gives you... time and headspace to think about what matters."
- The word "intentionally" appears in the thesis. That is the brand thread.

## CTA (about 30 seconds)

1. **One primary ask** from the [CTA registry](../funnel/cta-registry.md).
2. A soft line only if it fits ([LP vs OA](../brand/lp-vs-oa.md)).
3. **A comment question** tied to the exercise. ("What's the ONE repeated job you're handing to AI first?")
4. **Name the next video.** Do not say "thanks for watching, subscribe".
5. "See you in the next one." and an `[ON SCREEN TEXT]` repeating the link.

## The one-belief rule inside a script

The hook plants the problem. The lessons build the agreement. The recap states the belief. If a lesson does not serve the belief, cut it. The sample's belief: *AI used intentionally gives founders their time and headspace back.*

## Length control

| If the draft is... | Do |
|---|---|
| Over 1,900 words | Cut a lesson or merge two. Teach the second-best point first and the best second. |
| Under 1,400 words | Add one more honest-limit moment or one more named story. Not another lesson. |
| 5 lessons, each over 380 words | Cut the quote or Linh's version, not the exercise. |

## Mistakes the audit found (do not repeat)

- Opening on an abstract claim.
- A title that promises something the script delivers late.
- The channel pitch at 0:50.
- Two CTAs.
- The same story used in two videos within five weeks.
- A tenure number that differs from the fact sheet.
