# Titles

## What the data says

| Title type | CTR (first six videos) | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Names a cost** | 3.5% to 3.8% | "The 4 Hidden Costs of Doing Everything Yourself" (3.82%), "The Costly Mistake That Keeps Founders Busy" (3.48%) |
| Delegation and hiring | 1.8% to 1.9% | "5 Delegation Steps", "Don't Hire More People Until You Do This One Thing" |
| Abstract money framework | 2.16% | "Is a Business Expense Worth It" |

Titles that name a price the viewer is already paying get clicked. Titles that promise a mechanic hold viewers once they are in.

## The S / A / P gate

Every title is tested before filming. **Fail one and rewrite.**

| Gate | Question | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **S** Specificity | Does it contain a number, a name, a result or a villain? | Add "5", "3 Things", "100+ team", "Task Delegator" |
| **A** Action | Can the viewer do or avoid something tomorrow? | Add the verb: stop, delegate, count, delete |
| **P** Phone-feed | Would a tired founder stop scrolling? | Shorter, one clear idea, no jargon |

Examples from the schedule:

| Working title | Gate | Suggested title |
|---|---|---|
| Should You Hire a Virtual Assistant? | S fails | Should You Hire a Virtual Assistant? What a 100+ Person Team Taught Me |
| Managing a Remote Team? Stop Watching Them | S fails | I Run a 100+ Person Remote Team. I Don't Watch Any of Them. |
| Are You an Intentional Founder or an Accidental One? | S fails | Are You an Accidental Founder? 5 Signs You Built a Business You Didn't Choose |
| The 5 Pillars of 360° Success | P fails | I Measure My Life in 5 Scores, Not Revenue. Here's My Scorecard. |

## Title patterns that suit Linh

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| **The cost** | The 4 Hidden Costs of Doing Everything Yourself |
| **First person + business-size number** | I Run a 100+ Person Team Without Losing My Family |
| **Number + villain** | The 3 Things to Delegate First (It's Not Admin) |
| **"I stopped" confession** | I Stopped Approving Everything. Here's What Changed in My Week. |
| **Identity question** | Are You an Accidental Founder? 5 Signs... |
| **Contrarian** | Don't Hire More People Until You Do This One Thing |
| **System you can copy** | The Weekly Delegation Schedule I Give Every New Assistant |

First person plus a number plus a total outcome is the shape all the real competitor breakouts share.

## Rules

1. **Keywords stay in the problem.** delegate, bottleneck, remote team, virtual assistant, doing everything yourself. "Intentional founder" has no search demand, so it is the brand and appears in hook, thumbnail tag or close, not as the lead keyword.
2. **The title is a promise. Deliver it in the first minute.** The audit flagged "The Costly Mistake That Keeps Founders Busy" (really the 4 types of leverage) and "Don't Hire Until You Do This One Thing" (never names the one thing early).
3. **Under about 60 characters shows in full on mobile.** Put the hook words first.
4. **No clickbait the video cannot pay off.** If a title opens a loop, the hook must name it by second 10.
5. **Title and thumbnail say different things.** The thumbnail is 2 to 3 words of emotion. The title carries the specific. See [thumbnails.md](thumbnails.md).
6. **Do not use "freedom" as the hook word.** None of the breakouts did.
7. **No emojis. Title Case for the first clause, sentence case after a full stop.**
8. **A/B test two titles** when a slot allows. Record both in the video brief.

## Per video: produce

- 3 title options, ranked, each with gate result
- The one the team recommends and why
- A subtitle line for the description's first sentence (the same promise, longer)

The `hooks-and-titles` skill does this.
