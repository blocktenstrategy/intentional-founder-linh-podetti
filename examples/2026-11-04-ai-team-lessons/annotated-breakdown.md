# Annotated breakdown: How My Team Actually Uses AI (5 Lessons for Founders)

The reference script for the house template. Read [script.md](script.md) first, then this. Word count: about 1,720 words, 10 to 11 minutes at roughly 160 words a minute.

**Source:** Outsourcing Angel monthly huddle, Tue 3 Nov 2026, the AI experience share (Fathom recording).

## What the script does well (keep)

| Move | Where | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Opens on a small, real moment** | Hook, the dentist question | A person, a place, an action. No welcome. No pitch. The audit says stories beat claims. |
| **The promise is plain and early** | Hook, last line: "I'm passing on those lessons to you" | Confirms the title by second 25 |
| **One-line credibility, then the brand line** | Intro | "I help founders become Intentional Founders" plants the identity without a lecture |
| **Names the two camps** | Intro | The viewer picks themselves in 10 seconds |
| **"None of it was theory"** | Intro | Sets the proof standard: real screens, real before and after |
| **The same seven beats per lesson** | Lessons 1 to 5 | Predictable for the viewer, fast to write and QA |
| **Specific numbers** | Lesson 1: "about 17 hours a month", "about 80%" | Specificity (the S in the gate) |
| **The emotion under the number** | Lesson 1: "the biggest benefit wasn't just the time. It was her stress" | Disclosure and recognition, not a feature list |
| **A direct quote from the person** | All lessons | Proof that cannot be faked, a voice that is not Linh's |
| **Linh's own version** | "I do this myself too" | Disclosure before diagnosis |
| **An honest failure lesson** | Lesson 2 | "The most honest part of the whole huddle." Builds trust. |
| **Honest about effort** | Lesson 3: "took her more than a day to build... it's never finished" | Removes the "it should be easy" shame |
| **Every lesson ends with an exercise** | "Your exercise:" | A step the viewer can do tomorrow (the A in the gate) |
| **Recap in the imperative** | Recap | Five lines they can screenshot |
| **Thesis uses "intentionally"** | Recap last line | The brand thread, once, at the close |
| **No channel pitch** | Whole script | Matches the audit's top fix |
| **Comment question tied to the exercise** | CTA | "What's the ONE repeated job you're handing to AI first?" |

## Section by section

### Hook (0:00 to 0:25, about 120 words)

1. Moment: the dentist question while driving.
2. Result: it searched her email, she called them.
3. Proof of ease: "I didn't open a single app or message anyone in my team."
4. Widen: the team huddle, every department shared.
5. Promise.
6. `[ON SCREEN TEXT: Real lessons from a real team]`

### Intro (0:25 to 1:00, about 130 words)

- Name, company, book, the brand line.
- Two camps: haven't started (overwhelming), or played but nothing changed.
- "Here are five lessons."

### Lessons (1:00 to 9:45)

| # | Title | Time | The story | The exercise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Start with one boring, repeated job | 1:00–2:45 | Finance lead, Xero reports, about 17 hours a month | List three weekly tasks. Circle the one that drains you. |
| 2 | Treat AI like a direct report, not a magic button | 2:45–4:45 | Verify three times. Mistakes the team caught. Wording rules called "skills". | Write a "how I like it done" note. |
| 3 | Connect your tools so the work flows | 4:45–7:00 | Fathom → Notion → email draft. "All I do is, yes, book it." | Map every step and app after a meeting. Mark which need judgement. |
| 4 | Use AI to build capability, not just speed | 7:00–8:45 | Marketing: customer profiles, in-house paid ads, custom GPTs | Where are you waiting on outside expertise? |
| 5 | Make it a team habit, not a founder hobby | 8:45–9:45 | Monthly huddle share, buddies, "My morning brief is so plain!" | Book 30 minutes for an AI share. |

### Recap (9:45 to 10:15)

Five imperative lines, then the thesis: "AI won't give you your life back on its own. But used intentionally, it gives you and your team something precious: time and headspace to think about what matters."

### CTA (10:15 to 10:45)

1. Free 360-Degree Quiz.
2. Soft Outsourcing Angel line.
3. Comment question.
4. "See you in the next one."

## What to adjust against the current rules

The script is the template, and these are the places where the current rules differ. They are flagged, not changed.

| Issue | Why | Suggested fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Two asks** (quiz and Outsourcing Angel) | The audit says one CTA per video | Keep the quiz as the single ask, or swap to the calculator once it is live. Drop the OA line or fold it into the description. See [open question 4](../../docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md). |
| **No named next video** | The audit says point to one named next video | Replace "See you in the next one" with the next video's title |
| **Hook claims "I didn't open a single app"** | A strong claim | Keep, but confirm it is exactly what happened |
| **Lesson 2's "skills" jargon** | The viewer may not know the term | Add six words of definition ("wording rules the AI follows") when spoken |
| **Length 10 to 11 minutes** | The audit says 8 to 12 | Fine. Do not extend. |
| **Source is an OA team huddle** | LP vs OA | The stories are team stories, not clients. Acceptable. Confirm each team member agrees to be named or paraphrased. |
| **Titles and thumbnails** | Title options say "AI". The audit says problem keywords. | Test "Treat AI Like a Team Member, Not a Magic Button" against a cost-led title. See [titles](../../docs/youtube/titles.md). |
| **Thumbnail text options** | "AI IS YOUR DIRECT REPORT", "START WITH ONE REPORT", "WHAT AI GOT WRONG" | "WHAT AI GOT WRONG" names a cost. Test it first. |
