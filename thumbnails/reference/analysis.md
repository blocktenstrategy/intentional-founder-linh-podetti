## What is consistent (keep)

- 16:9, Linh on the right, face large, usually 40 to 50% of the frame height
- 2 to 3 words of heavy sans-serif type, white, left side
- **One burnt-orange accent**, either a filled label or an underline bar
- Dark, warm background with a gradient behind the text
- One concrete prop
- Italic serif for the soft word ("You'll", "They Act Like", "runs")

## What changed over time

- The oldest two (8 and 11) use a whiteboard in a light setting.
- The mid set (6 to 9) uses a dark, moody background and an emotional face.
- The newest (1 and 2) use a **paired template**: an orange label, a phone or chat prop, the same face placement.
- The newest Short (3) is warmer and lighter, outdoors, with no prop. This is the first thumbnail that shows life, not work.

## What the CTR says

- The two highest CTRs (3.82%, 3.48%) both have **a cost word** ("Not worth it", "WORKING TOO MUCH").
- The three lowest (1.80 to 1.87%) have a **feeling** ("REGRET THIS", "CAN'T KEEP UP?", "I DO EVERYTHING") but no price.
- Tiny sample: 6 videos, 7,205 impressions across all of them. Use as a test hypothesis: **name a cost, not only a feeling**.

## Inconsistencies to fix

| Issue | Videos | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Orange label vs orange underline | 1 and 2 vs the rest | Label for questions and A/B pairs, underline for statements |
| Text does not name a subject | "The best ROI", "THIS REPLACED ME" | Add the object or the cost |
| No prop | 3, 7 | Add one where the topic allows, or keep as the "story" template |
| Face expression is generic | 1, 2 | Shoot the expression for the specific script |
| Brand not visible | all | Introduce "ACCIDENTAL / INTENTIONAL" as the orange word in identity videos |
